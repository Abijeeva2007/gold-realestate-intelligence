Executive Question: Will US Home Prices Continue to Increase?

Yes, home prices are projected to rise at +4.6% over the next 12 months and +9.8% over 24 months. While elevated mortgage rates (6.65%) have reduced total transaction volume, home prices remain strongly supported by three structural macroeconomic forces: (1) The "Mortgage Rate-Lock Effect" where 78% of US mortgage holders have fixed rates below 5%, preventing existing homes from hitting the market; (2) A structural national deficit of 3.2M single-family homes created by 15 years of underbuilding since 2008; and (3) The Federal Reserve's rate-easing cycle, which lowers borrowing costs and unlocks pent-up buyer demand.

1. Expected Total Returns (CAGR)

8.5% – 11.2% Total Annual Return for residential real estate, composed of 4.6%–5.5% capital appreciation + 4.0%–5.8% gross rental yields (Cap Rates).

2. Top High-Conviction Growth Corridors

Sunbelt & In-Migration Hubs: Florida (Miami, Tampa, Orlando), Texas (Dallas, Austin, Houston), Georgia (Atlanta), and North Carolina (Raleigh, Charlotte) continue to lead in population and job growth.

3. Single-Family (SFR) vs Multi-Family

Single-Family Homes: Best long-term equity appreciation and lowest tenant turnover. 2–4 Unit Multi-Family: Highest cash-on-cash yield and strongest Debt-Service Coverage Ratios (DSCR).
