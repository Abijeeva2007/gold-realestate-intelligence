🛡️ Section 4: Data Quality, Provenance & Non-Fabrication Audit

Transparent disclosure of verified vs unverified jurisdictions, legal source provenance, and strict segregation rules.

Audited Nov 5, 2026

Verified Source Connectors

• TNREGINET: Official Gazette Guideline registers published by the Inspector General of Registration, Government of Tamil Nadu. Covers all 38 districts.

• NHB RESIDEX: Quarterly Housing Price Indices published by the National Housing Bank (RBI subsidiary) for Chennai and Coimbatore.

• RBI DBIE: Central bank monetary aggregates: Repo Rate (5.75%), Housing WALR (7.85%), Gross Housing Credit (₹30.85L Cr).

• BIS: Bank for International Settlements Residential Property Price Statistics.

• TNRERA: Statutory registry of approved developments under the RERA Act 2016.

Non-Negotiable Integrity Safeguards

✅ Zero Guideline Conflation: Statutory guideline values are strictly treated as minimum tax floors and never mixed into market transaction calculations.

✅ Category Purity: Residential apartments, residential plots, commercial properties, and agricultural lands are never blended into a single composite.

✅ Anti-Fabrication Error Handling: Unmonitored rural revenue tracts return an explicit INSUFFICIENT_DATA response rather than synthetic prices.

✅ Chronological Split: Machine learning models are evaluated exclusively on future holdouts to prevent lookahead leakage.
